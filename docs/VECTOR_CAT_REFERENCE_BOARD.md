# Vector Cat Reference Board

## Purpose

This board locks the mascot to the owner's supplied Cat Quest examples instead of drifting back toward realistic cat anatomy. References are for proportion, silhouette, expression, and motion analysis only; shipping artwork remains an original urban code-native design.

## Selected references

- The four Cat Quest character images supplied during the 2026-08-22 design review are the primary proportion reference: oversized rounded head, tiny upright torso, two short legs, two readable arms, compact feet, and costume-driven silhouette.
- [Cat Quest — The Gentlebros](https://thegentlebros.com/catquest/): secondary reference for abstraction level, compact proportions, cheek construction, and readable equipment layering. Do not copy a character, costume, palette, or facial shape.
- [Cat profile silhouette collection](https://www.vectorstock.com/royalty-free-vector/cat-silhouettes-head-cute-feline-faces-vector-25946950): forehead, nose, muzzle, and jaw relationships in a right-facing profile. Used only to diagnose anatomy; the realistic neck construction was explicitly rejected for the mascot.
- [Running Cat — Openclipart](https://openclipart.org/detail/258775/running-cat): anatomy counter-reference only. It is explicitly not the locomotion or body-plan target.
- [LongCat / CopyCat — Franziska Volmer](https://dribbble.com/shots/25862664-LongCat-CopyCat): elongated graphic silhouette and limited brand palette.
- [Angry Cat — Franziska Volmer](https://dribbble.com/shots/25728369-Angry-Cat): anger that remains playful, readable, and extensible across a brand system.
- [Spooky Friends — Banessa Millet](https://dribbble.com/shots/14478293-Spooky-friends-walking-around): scrappy character energy and accessories that belong to the character instead of floating above it.
- [Cat run cycle — Gaanya Singh](https://www.behance.net/gallery/167418867/Animation-Cats-run-cycle): timing reference for contact, compression, passing, and flight only; translate those beats into a bipedal chibi jog.
- [Cat character turnaround](https://lchinbfajournal.wordpress.com/2016/02/01/final-character-designs/): consistent skull and body construction across profile and three-quarter views.
- [Pixel cat mascot redesign — Iana Suvorova](https://www.behance.net/gallery/184530789/Pixel-cat-mascot-redesign): simplifying an established mascot while preserving its recognizable character.

## Adopted construction rules

- Use an oversized rounded sticker head on a tiny upright torso. Do not render a literal neck, throat fold, side-ear plate, realistic panther profile, or quadruped body.
- The mascot is always bipedal: exactly two arms and two legs, with a bomber/hoodie and sneakers establishing the urban silhouette.
- Drive limbs from compact hip and shoulder zones. Elbows and knees must bend clearly enough to read at phone scale.
- Aim the skull and muzzle into the run direction. Keep the far eye smaller and quieter than the near eye so the face reads as angled rather than camera-facing.
- Both ears must rise from the top head contour; neither may read as a growth attached to the side of the face.
- Judge every pose as a black silhouette before adding skin markings or cosmetics.
- Use six authored biped run keys covering opposing contact, compression, passing, and flight. Smooth-step between strong keys so the stride has impact without jitter.
- Keep the mascot compact at gameplay scale and magnify it only in title and wardrobe presentation.
- Maintain named mouth, eye, brow, ear, hat, glasses, shoulder, and paw zones.

## Explicit rejects

- front-facing square head on a side-facing body;
- potato or pill torso;
- mirrored legs;
- any return to a four-legged run, feline ribcage, shoulder hump, or long horizontal body;
- realistic panther anatomy, explicit neck folds, or a pasted secondary ear;
- adding a raised smoking arm without removing its normal near-arm swing;
- fantasy armor, medieval tunics, capes, swords, or Cat Quest character copies;
- cosmetics used to hide weak anatomy;
- copying the shapes, markings, or identity of any single reference.
- rounded neon app-shell UI, capsule buttons, and generic cyberpunk gradients.
